## Bounded Sui RPC projection

`src/transport/sui-proto.ts` is a handwritten, adapted and modified bounded
TypeScript projection of the Mysten Labs Sui RPC v2 schema. It is limited to
the three read-only unary methods implemented by this package and omits
unrelated schema fields. The source is pinned to Mysten Labs `sui-apis`
commit `aa9ba2c13b2a9348059b38110e64af39849a0377` and the affected upstream
paths are:

- [`ledger_service.proto`](https://github.com/MystenLabs/sui-apis/blob/aa9ba2c13b2a9348059b38110e64af39849a0377/proto/sui/rpc/v2/ledger_service.proto)
